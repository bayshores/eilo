Connect your Chrome activity.

Choose which study sites eïlo can notice. Opening this guide does not install the extension or start sharing.

  1. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode if you choose to install this local, unpacked extension.
  2. Choose Load unpacked and select activity/extension (inside your eïlo checkout).
  3. Open the eïlo extension's popup. Select the study sites you want to share and choose Grant selected sites. The available sites are LeetCode, NeetCode and Python documentation.
  4. Open the Chrome connection page in Chrome and choose Connect Chrome. The installed extension is detected automatically. Keep that connection page open while sharing.

What sharing allows

The extension reads the active tab of the focused Chrome window on the study sites you grant. Chrome withholds unapproved sites’ URLs and titles. Paths, queries and fragments are removed locally. Selected observations may be sent to Luna and saved with your Hermes conversation.

Access details

Chrome’s host grants can support broader capabilities than these reads. This extension does not change tabs or pages and has no content scripts, browser-history, cookies, screenshot or keystroke collection.

To stop: use Pause or Turn off on the connection page, close that page, or revoke sites in the extension popup. You can also remove the extension through Chrome’s extensions page.
